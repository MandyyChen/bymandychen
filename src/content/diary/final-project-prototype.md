## final project: rough physical prototype

### the prototype

before building any electronics, i made a rough lightstick out of cardboard, tape, paper, and cotton balls, to test how people would hold it and what they'd naturally do with it.

i also used ai to generate a cleaner technical diagram from my sketch: globe, mvm logo insert, handle with an inner decorative insert, power button, usb-c port, battery cap, strap hole, and a wrist strap.

### prototype v2

for class, i made two lightsticks so i could test the interactions between sticks: black paper rolled into handles, with wax paper globes over pink and white fillers to diffuse the light.

### testing

i had people listen to music while holding it.

### what i observed

without any instructions, people already squeezed it, swung it, and moved it to the music. shake to cheer and tap to share build on movements people already make with a lightstick.
