title: a walk to the river
place: new haven
---

Left the house without deciding where I was going, which is the only way
I ever actually leave. The front door sticks in warm weather; you have to
lean on it. Down the block the sidewalk was still dark from someone's
sprinkler and the whole street smelled like wet stone.

Went under the overpass where the pigeons live. There is a particular
sound they make lifting off all at once, a soft clatter, and then the
larger silence afterward that always feels like it belongs to me.

A dog watched me from a porch without barking. I watched it back. Neither
of us moved for long enough that it started to feel like an agreement.

The river was lower than last week, brown and quiet, no boats. I stood
there until the light went flat and then turned around and walked home
the long way, past the shut market, the phone in my pocket buzzing with
things that could wait.
