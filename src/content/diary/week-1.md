- Left the house without deciding where I was going — the only way I ever
  actually leave. Front door sticks in warm weather.
- Sidewalk still dark from someone's sprinkler; the whole street smelled
  like wet stone.
- Went under the overpass where the pigeons live. The clatter when they
  lift off all at once, then the bigger silence after.
- A dog watched me from a porch without barking. I watched it back. Long
  enough that it started to feel like an agreement.
- The river was low, brown and quiet, no boats. Stood there until the
  light went flat.
- Walked home the long way, past the shut market, phone buzzing with
  things that could wait.
